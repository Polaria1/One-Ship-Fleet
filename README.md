# One Ship Fleet

An upgrade-focused Endless Sky mod for those who like a little more modularity and extracting every bit of power and utility possible out of a single ship.
<br>
Have you ever fought a marauder ship and wondered mid-fight: "You know, why is it that some Back Alley Pirates can directly modify the shield matrix and hull of their ship for a permanent advantage, yet no matter how much money I, The Player That Saves Humanity, throw at a ship, I'll never have that same ability? Why can't I have an advantage like they can?"
Well, wonder no longer! One Ship Fleet aims to add a variety of upgrades that are unique, have a remote pretense of balance, and make your life as a solo or minimally escorted starship captain just that bit easier! Upgrade capabilities may vary from ship-to-ship, rewarding exploration and knowledge of Endless Sky's lore. A shiny new Falcon might already be in tip-top shape, but don't you think a half-milennia old Firebird might deserve some TLC?
<br>
# Features:
- Omnis support
- Armor plating kits that can strengthen hull, add mobility, or provide utility
- Outfits that change the dynamics of a build, offering niche advantages with drawbacks
- Chassis overhauls for certain ships that provide a permanent upgrade to capacity and hull. Don't expect rebuilding a ship from scratch to be cheap, though!
